The color system blends warm earth tones with soft, luminous pinks to create an inviting, energetic, yet grounded aesthetic. 

### Color Breakdown & Meaning

* **Gradient Pink (`#FF6767` to `#FFD1D1`) – *Hero Accent (60%)***
  * **Meaning & Mood:** Radiates vitality, warmth, and fresh energy. Transitioning from coral rose to a delicate blush, this gradient serves as the primary visual anchor, softening the composition while instantly catching the viewer's attention.
* **Warm Terracotta / Orange (`#DC5E3C` & `#DE9C79`) – *Warmth & Energy (10%)***
  * **Meaning & Mood:** Evokes roasted spices, autumn warmth, and optimism. It adds a punchy, vibrant kick that bridges the intensity of the pink gradient with the grounded tones.
* **Pale Honey Yellow (`#EAC074`) – *Cheer & Brightness (10%)***
  * **Meaning & Mood:** Represents morning light, sweet cream, and approachability. It acts as an uplifting highlight that keeps the layout friendly and dynamic.
* **Deep Roast Brown (`#8C492F`) – *Grounding Anchor (10%)***
  * **Meaning & Mood:** Symbolizes rich espresso, organic soil, and depth. It provides necessary weight and high-contrast structural definition against the lighter tones.
* **Oat Milk / Light Grey (`#E3DBD0`) – *Neutral Canvas (10%)***
  * **Meaning & Mood:** A warm, muted stone-beige that delivers breathing room. It eliminates harsh starkness, ensuring the surrounding colors interact harmoniously without eye fatigue.

The typographic hierarchy balances expressive retro display lettering, authoritative uppercase styling, free-flowing script, and classic editorial serif.

### Typeface Rationale & Pairing

1. **Ahsing (Retro Display Serif)**
   * **Style:** High-contrast, voluptuous, retro-psychedelic curves with fluid, dramatic serifs.
   * **Role & Impact:** Acts as the emotional centerpiece and hero display font. Its bold, wave-like letterforms bring immediate personality, creative flair, and vintage charm to headlines.
2. **Copperplate Gothic 29 BC (Structured All-Caps Sans/Glyphic)**
   * **Style:** Wide-set, geometric, refined glyphic lettering with subtle, sharp terminal serifs.
   * **Role & Impact:** Delivers authoritative grounding. Used in full uppercase, it cuts through the soft, organic curves of Ahsing and the scripts to provide clean, crisp architectural structure for headers and metadata.
3. **Brittany & Angeletta (Expressive Calligraphy & Handwritten Script)**
   * **Style:** Organic, delicate, continuous-stroke cursive with signature monoline elegance.
   * **Role & Impact:** Injects intimacy and personal warmth. These scripts mimic spontaneous handwritten notes, creating an inviting, handcrafted human touch that breaks rigid grids.
4. **Libre Baskerville (Classic Editorial Serif)**
   * **Style:** Optimized transitional serif with generous counters, sturdy serifs, and high readability at small scales.
   * **Role & Impact:** The workhorse for subheadings and body copy. It brings editorial credibility, balance, and effortless legibility, ensuring the design remains polished and informative beneath its creative accents.
