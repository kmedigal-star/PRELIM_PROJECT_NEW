## How I Came Up With the Concept 🏃‍♂️🥦

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/UUGwpFiOnZi86FXXgq/giphy.gif" alt="BuzzFeed New Year GIF" width="480" />
</p>

* **Topic Choice & Problem Space:** Modern life is engineered for prolonged stillness—desk-bound work, long study grinds, motor transport, and screen addictions have quietly turned sedentary behavior into a global silent epidemic. Rather than presenting generic health warnings, the concept introduces an innovative intervention: the **"MOVE-TO-NOURISH" Smart Kiosk**, an interactive station that converts physical movement into immediate tangible rewards.
* **Target Audience:** College students, desk-bound professionals, and urban workers who spend most waking hours seated. These individuals already know they should move more, but experience cognitive overload, decision fatigue, and lack of immediate positive reinforcement.
* **The Core Premise:** Awareness alone does not alter routine; incentivized action does. By laying out the clinical reality (definition, global prevalence, systemic physical costs, and behavioral drivers) and culminating in a concrete technological solution, the infographic transitions the reader directly from problem awareness to actionable change.

## Design Choices 🎨📐

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/TITLn6h5CtL9G9mWh3/giphy.gif" alt="Whylder broccoli sticker" width="366" />
</p>

* **Zig-Zag "S-Curve" Narrative Flow:** The structure alternates back and forth between left-aligned and right-aligned text cards (Steps 1 through 5). This prevents monotonous vertical reading and naturally snakes the viewer's eyes through every stage of the narrative.
* **Clinical Green & Earth Warmth Palette:**
  * **Forest & Olive Green (`#4A7C3B` / `#82A737`):** Dominates the banner and numbered badge pills, signifying vitality, growth, recovery, and nourishing health.
  * **Warm Cream & Sand Background:** Replaces harsh digital white with an organic, soothing beige tone that reduces eye strain and complements health-related imagery.
  * **Vibrant Accent Tones (Crimson & Amber):** Highlights systemic risks—such as the anatomical heart graphic showing arterial plaque build-up—to signal critical medical urgency without panic.
* **Visual Chunking with Rounded Cards:** Information is packaged into clean, white rounded containers paired with numbered block badges (1 through 5) set in a bold, outlined slab serif font. This provides clear cognitive pacing and immediate readability.
* **Direct Visual Iconography:** Every data block is anchored by custom illustrative storytelling:
  * *Step 1:* A reclined character surrounded by takeout junk food, clutter, and screens.
  * *Step 2:* A clipboard diet plan, measuring tape, and dumbell.
  * *Step 3:* An anatomical heart with an arterial cross-section spotlighting vascular damage (atherosclerosis).
  * *Step 4:* An overwhelmed worker engulfed by clocks, floating devices, and paperwork.
  * *Step 5:* An approachable health facilitator surrounded by fresh fruit, clean hydration, scales, and mobile wellness tracking.

## Creative Process 🛠️✨

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/3ohjV0MRFCJORyUQec/giphy.gif" alt="Hungry food gif" width="480" />
</p>

1. **Epidemiological Research & Distillation:** Gathered data on sedentary trends (including WHO estimates noting that 60%–85% of adults live sedentary lives and 1 in 3 are physically inactive) and synthesized complex pathologies into clear clinical bullet points (Type 2 DM, DVT, CVD, obesity).
2. **Concept Engineering (The Smart Kiosk):** Brainstormed an actionable micro-intervention: transforming traditional calorie-dense vending machines into interactive wellness hubs where movement sensors unlock fresh food and store vouchers.
3. **Drafting & Hierarchy Mapping:** Outlined a 5-step instructional arc—*Definition → Prevalence → Effects → External Factors → Solution*. This ensures the reader fully grasps the severity of the condition before presenting the kiosk innovation.
4. **Art Direction & Balancing Tone:** Balanced solemn clinical warnings with approachable, motivating lifestyle art. Soft, organic line work and warm tones make the subject feel actionable and empowering rather than strictly punitive.
5. **Core Meaning:** The piece reflects the belief that wellness should not be an inaccessible luxury or another app notification to ignore. *"Nourish Your Body, Energize Your Life"* encapsulates a shift toward embedding proactive health directly into everyday public spaces.
